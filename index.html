About the Initiative

The Future Skills Initiative aims to support high school and early university students by providing accessible, hands-on technology education. We believe every student deserves the chance to build the digital literacy needed to thrive in today's world.

Many students lack guidance and resources when it comes to learning modern digital skills. There is often a gap between what schools teach and what the job market demands. This initiative exists to help bridge that gap through practical, approachable learning experiences.
